---
title: "Hosted checkout"
description: "Redirect the shopper to a Kalixo-hosted payment page."
icon: "window-maximize"
---

Every checkout session has a `url` on `checkout.kalixo.io`. Redirect the shopper there
when you do not want to build a payment page.

```javascript
const session = await kalixo.checkoutSessions.create({
  amount: 2500,
  currency: 'GBP',
  country: 'GB',
  reference: 'order-2002',
  return_url: 'https://shop.example.com/checkout/complete',
});
res.redirect(303, session.url);
```

- The URL contains the session's client secret after `#`. Browsers never send that part
  to servers. Treat the URL as private to the shopper.
- After the payment, the shopper returns to your `return_url` with `?session_id=cs_...`.
  Confirm the outcome on your server.
- If the payment is declined, the shopper can retry on the hosted page until the session expires.
- The page shows your account name, the amount and Kalixo's terms, because Kalixo is the
  merchant of record.

## When to choose the hosted checkout

| Hosted checkout | Kalixo.js |
| --- | --- |
| No frontend work | Checkout inside your own page |
| Shopper leaves your site briefly | Shopper stays on your site |
| Same payment methods and security | Same payment methods and security |
