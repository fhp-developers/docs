---
title: "Reports"
description: "CSV exports for reconciliation and for invoicing your share to Kalixo."
icon: "file-csv"
---

Kalixo collects the payments as merchant of record. Use the `financial_transactions`
report as the basis for invoicing your share to Kalixo.

```javascript
const report = await kalixo.reports.create({ type: 'financial_transactions', period_start: '2026-10-01T00:00:00Z', period_end: '2026-11-01T00:00:00Z' });
// Poll until ready (usually seconds), then download.
const ready = await kalixo.reports.retrieve(report.id);
if (ready.status === 'ready') {
  const csv = await kalixo.reports.download(report.id);
}
```

| Type | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| `financial_transactions` | Every capture, refund, chargeback and chargeback reversal in the period |
| `payments` | Payments created in the period with their amounts and status |
| `refunds` | Refunds created in the period |
| `disputes` | Disputes opened in the period |

- `period_end` is exclusive; a report covers at most 92 days.
- Amounts appear twice: `amount_minor` (integer) and `amount` (decimal string).
- Reports are kept for 30 days.
