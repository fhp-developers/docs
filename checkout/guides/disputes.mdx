When a shopper disputes a payment with their bank, you receive `dispute.created`.
Kalixo is merchant of record and handles the dispute with the card scheme; you
provide the evidence.

| Event | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `dispute.created` | A dispute or information request was opened (`status: needs_response`) |
| `dispute.funds_withdrawn` | The disputed amount was debited |
| `dispute.updated` | Kalixo submitted a defense or the dispute changed |
| `dispute.won` | The dispute was decided in Kalixo's favour; funds are returned |
| `dispute.lost` | The dispute was lost or accepted |

## Upload evidence

Upload evidence before `evidence_due_by`.

```javascript
await kalixo.disputes.uploadEvidence('dp_...', {
  document_type: 'proof_of_delivery',
  file_name: 'delivery-order-1001.pdf',
  content_type: 'application/pdf',
  content_base64: fs.readFileSync('delivery.pdf').toString('base64'),
  description: 'Code delivered to the shopper account on 12 October',
});
```

- Formats: PDF (max 2 MB), JPEG or TIFF (max 10 MB).
- Never upload card numbers or identity documents.
- Useful evidence for digital goods: delivery logs, account activity after delivery,
  shopper communication and your terms accepted at checkout.

While a dispute is open the payment status is `disputed`; refunds are blocked.
