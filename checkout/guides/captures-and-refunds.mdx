## Automatic or manual capture

By default, payments are captured automatically: you receive `payment.authorized`
and then `payment.captured`.

Set `capture_method: "manual"` on the checkout session to authorise first and capture
later, for example when you ship.

```javascript
await kalixo.payments.capture('pay_...', { amount: 600 }); // partial capture
await kalixo.payments.capture('pay_...');                  // the remaining amount
```

- A capture is `pending` until `payment.captured`, `payment.partially_captured` or `capture.failed` arrives.
- The total captured can never exceed the authorised amount (`amount_too_large`).
- Not every payment method supports separate or partial captures. Authorisations expire
  after a few days depending on the method; capture as soon as you can.

## Cancel

```javascript
await kalixo.payments.cancel('pay_...');
```

Cancels an authorised payment that has not been captured. You receive `payment.canceled`.

## Refund

```javascript
await kalixo.refunds.create({ payment: 'pay_...', amount: 300, reason: 'requested_by_customer' });
await kalixo.refunds.create({ payment: 'pay_...' }); // the rest of the captured amount
```

- Multiple partial refunds are allowed. The total refunded can never exceed the captured
  amount, even when requests arrive at the same time.
- A refund is `pending` until `refund.succeeded` or `refund.failed`.
- Disputed payments cannot be refunded (`payment_disputed`); the dispute decides the outcome.
- With automatic capture, wait for `payment.captured` before refunding.
