---
title: "Amount integrity"
description: "The amount you send is exactly the amount Kalixo charges."
icon: "shield-check"
---

- **You set the amount, only from your server.** `amount` and `currency` come from
  `POST /v1/checkout-sessions` with your secret key. The browser can never change them.
- **The amount locks when the shopper starts paying.** You can `PATCH` `amount`,
  `currency` and `line_items` until the first payment attempt; after that the API
  returns `amount_locked`. Expire the session and create a new one if the order changes.
- **Line items must add up.** When you send `line_items`, the sum of
  `quantity * unit_amount` must equal `amount` (`line_items_mismatch` otherwise). See
  [Line items](#line-items) for tax, shipping and discounts.
- **Every provider event is checked.** If the provider ever reports a different amount,
  Kalixo sets `amount_mismatch: true`, sends `payment.amount_mismatch`, and cancels or
  refunds the payment automatically. Never fulfil a payment with `amount_mismatch: true`.
- **Confirm on your server, from Kalixo's records.** Do not fulfil on browser events,
  `return_url` redirects, or `checkout_session.status` alone. Fulfil only after
  `payment.captured` (or `payment.authorized` for manual capture) and
  `amount_mismatch` is `false` on `GET /v1/payments/{id}`.
- **Totals are enforced.** Captures never exceed the authorised amount and refunds never
  exceed the captured amount, even under concurrent requests.
- **Everything is echoed back.** Payments and events carry `amount`, `currency`,
  `amount_authorized`, `amount_captured`, `amount_refunded`, `amount_disputed`, your
  `reference` and your `metadata`, unchanged.

## Minor units

| Currency | Example | `amount` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EUR, GBP, USD, SAR, AED | 49.99 | `4999` |
| KWD, BHD, OMR, JOD | 1.500 | `1500` |
| JPY | 1500 | `1500` |

## Supported currencies

Kalixo Checkout accepts these ISO 4217 currencies. Your account can be limited to some of
them (`currency_not_enabled`); ask Kalixo integrations which ones are enabled for you.

| Minor unit | Currencies |
| --- | --- |
| 2 decimals | AED, AUD, BRL, CAD, CHF, CZK, DKK, EGP, EUR, GBP, HKD, HUF, ILS, INR, MAD, MXN, NOK, NZD, PLN, QAR, RON, SAR, SEK, SGD, TRY, USD, ZAR |
| 3 decimals | BHD, JOD, KWD, OMR |
| 0 decimals | JPY |

`country` is the shopper's country as an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code. Which payment methods
the checkout offers depends on your account, currency and country; ask Kalixo integrations.

## Line items

`line_items` are optional. Kalixo stores them on the session and sends them to the payment
provider with the payment; they never change what Kalixo charges. When you send them:

- `unit_amount` is the price of **one unit, including tax**, in the minor unit.
- `tax_amount` is the tax **included** in one unit's `unit_amount`. It is informational:
  Kalixo does not calculate tax.
- `tax_percentage` is in **basis points** (hundredths of a percent): 21% is `2100`, 5.5% is
  `550`. This is different from the Distribution API, where `discountPercentage` is a plain
  percentage (`5` is 5%).
- The sum of `quantity * unit_amount` over all lines must equal `amount` exactly.

Model shipping and discounts as lines with a `type`:

| `type` | `unit_amount` | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `item` (default) | Zero or positive | Products |
| `shipping` | Zero or positive | Delivery or handling fees |
| `discount` | Zero or negative | Coupons, promotions, store credit |

Example: two EUR 25.00 gift cards with 21% VAT, EUR 2.99 delivery and a EUR 5.00 coupon.
Amounts are in minor units, so the total is 2 × 2500 + 299 − 500 = `4799` (EUR 47.99):

```json
{
  "amount": 4799,
  "currency": "EUR",
  "country": "NL",
  "reference": "order-1002",
  "return_url": "https://shop.example.com/checkout/complete",
  "line_items": [
    { "name": "Gift card EUR 25", "quantity": 2, "unit_amount": 2500, "tax_amount": 434, "tax_percentage": 2100 },
    { "name": "Delivery", "quantity": 1, "unit_amount": 299, "type": "shipping" },
    { "name": "Spring promotion", "quantity": 1, "unit_amount": -500, "tax_amount": -87, "type": "discount" }
  ]
}
```

A discount line's `tax_amount` is zero or negative and never larger than its `unit_amount`
in absolute value. A negative `unit_amount` on any other line fails with `line_items_invalid`.
