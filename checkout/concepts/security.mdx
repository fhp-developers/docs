Kalixo is the merchant of record and operates the payment providers, so most of the payment
security work is on Kalixo's side. A few things stay your responsibility. This page lists
both.

## PCI DSS

Card details are entered in secure fields that Kalixo.js loads from the payment provider.
Card data never touches your page or Kalixo's servers. This keeps your PCI scope at SAQ A,
provided you load Kalixo.js from `https://js.kalixo.io`. The hosted checkout keeps you at
SAQ A too.

<Warning>
  Do not bundle, copy, proxy or self-host Kalixo.js, and never build your own card fields.
  Either brings card data into your PCI scope.
</Warning>

## Keys and secrets

| Secret | Where it lives | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secret key (`kc_sk_...`) | Your server only | Never in browser or mobile code. Kalixo.js refuses it |
| Publishable key (`kc_pk_...`) | The browser | Works only from your registered domains (`origin_not_allowed` otherwise) |
| `client_secret` | Passed to Kalixo.js | Do not log it or send it to analytics. In the hosted checkout `url` it is in the fragment after `#`, which browsers never send to servers |
| Webhook secret (`whsec_...`) | Your server | Shown once, when you create or rotate it. Rotation signs with both secrets for 24 hours |

Kalixo stores only a hash of your API keys and shows each key once. To rotate a secret key,
deploy a new one, then ask Kalixo to revoke the old one. Revocation takes effect within 30
seconds.

## What Kalixo enforces

- **Amounts.** Kalixo charges only the amount of the checkout session and checks every
  amount the provider reports. See [Amount integrity](/checkout/concepts/amount-integrity).
- **Return domains.** A `return_url` must be on a domain registered for your account
  (`return_url_not_allowed` otherwise), so a leaked secret key cannot send shoppers to a
  look-alike site.
- **Webhook targets.** Webhook endpoints must be public HTTPS URLs. Private and internal
  addresses are refused (`url_not_public`).
- **Card testing.** Payment attempts are limited per session, per shopper IP address and per
  account. See [Rate limits](/checkout/concepts/rate-limits).
- **Hosted checkout.** The page shows order details only with the client secret.

## What you must do

- **Verify webhooks** against the raw body, and reject a timestamp older than 5 minutes.
  See [Webhooks](/checkout/concepts/webhooks#verify-the-signature).
- **Confirm on your server before you fulfil.** Read the payment with
  `GET /v1/payments/{id}`. See [Fulfilment](/checkout/guides/fulfilment).
- **Protect your pages.** Serve your checkout and return pages over HTTPS with
  `Strict-Transport-Security` and a Content Security Policy that allows Kalixo.js. See
  [Kalixo.js](/checkout/guides/kalixo-js).
- **Show shoppers only the decline `message`.** It is written to be safe to display.

## Data handling

- Kalixo encrypts shopper email addresses and names at rest. You never receive card numbers,
  so never store them.
- Never upload card numbers or identity documents as [dispute evidence](/checkout/guides/disputes).
- Payments and events return your `reference` and `metadata` unchanged. Do not put secrets or
  personal data in them.
- Events are kept for 30 days (`GET /v1/events`). Report files are kept for 30 days.

## Report a vulnerability

Email [integrations@kalixo.io](mailto:integrations@kalixo.io) with the details and, where
relevant, a `request_id`. Do not include real card numbers or personal data.
