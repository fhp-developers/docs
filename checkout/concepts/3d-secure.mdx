Many card payments need the shopper to confirm the payment with their bank: 3D Secure, which
Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) requires in Europe. The card issuer decides when a
challenge is needed. Kalixo.js and the hosted checkout run the challenge for you, so you do
not integrate 3D Secure yourself.

## How it works

- When a payment needs authentication, its status becomes `requires_action` and the
  checkout shows the challenge: a passcode, an approval in the banking app, or a biometric
  check.
- When the shopper passes, the payment moves to `authorized`. When they fail, it returns to
  `requires_payment_method` and the shopper can try again on the same session.
- Authentication is per attempt. Each attempt counts towards the
  [attempt limit](/checkout/concepts/rate-limits) of the session.

## What you handle

Nothing specific to 3D Secure. Handle the normal outcomes:

- In Kalixo.js, `payment.processing` means the outcome is still pending and
  `payment.failed` carries a `message` that is safe to show to the shopper.
- Confirm every order on your server before you deliver it. See
  [Fulfilment](/checkout/guides/fulfilment).

<Warning>
  A passed challenge is not a captured payment. It only allows the authorisation. Fulfil
  after `payment.captured` (or `payment.authorized` with manual capture) and
  `amount_mismatch: false`.
</Warning>

## Abandoned challenges

If the shopper closes the page during a challenge, the attempt stays open while the bank may
still answer. A new attempt on the same session fails with `payment_in_progress` until the
open attempt ends, after at most 30 minutes. To let the shopper pay sooner, create a new
checkout session.

## Decline codes

A failed authentication sets `decline_code` on `last_payment_error` and in `payment.failed`:

| `decline_code` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `authentication_failed` | The shopper did not pass the challenge |
| `authentication_required` | The issuer requires authentication and it was not completed |

See [Errors](/checkout/concepts/errors#decline-codes) for every decline code.

## Testing

Pay with `4000 0000 0000 3220` to get a test challenge, then approve or decline it in the
test dialog. See [Testing](/checkout/concepts/testing#test-cards).

| Test | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Approve the challenge | `payment.authorized`, then `payment.captured`; the order is fulfilled once |
| Decline the challenge | `payment.failed` with `authentication_failed`; the session stays open; nothing is fulfilled |

[Simulated payments](/checkout/concepts/testing#simulate-a-payment) skip 3D Secure. Use the
test card to test the challenge.
