`data.object` is always a full snapshot. Some events add `data.related`, for example
the payment of a refund event.

| Event | Object | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `checkout_session.completed` | checkout session | The session's payment was authorised |
| `checkout_session.expired` | checkout session | The session expired without a payment |
| `payment.authorized` | payment | The payment was authorised |
| `payment.captured` | payment | The full amount was captured (automatic capture sends this right after `payment.authorized`) |
| `payment.partially_captured` | payment | Part of a manual-capture payment was captured |
| `payment.failed` | payment | An attempt was declined; the shopper can retry while the session is open |
| `payment.canceled` | payment | An authorisation was canceled |
| `payment.expired` | payment | The payment or its last attempt expired |
| `payment.amount_updated` | payment | The authorised amount changed |
| `payment.amount_mismatch` | payment | The provider reported a different amount; Kalixo reverses the payment. Do not fulfil |
| `payment.fraud_reported` | payment | The issuer reported fraud; a dispute may follow |
| `capture.failed` | capture | A capture was rejected |
| `refund.succeeded` | refund | A refund succeeded |
| `refund.failed` | refund | A refund failed or was reversed |
| `dispute.created` | dispute | A dispute or information request was opened |
| `dispute.updated` | dispute | The dispute changed (for example a defense was submitted) |
| `dispute.funds_withdrawn` | dispute | The disputed amount was debited |
| `dispute.won` | dispute | The dispute was won |
| `dispute.lost` | dispute | The dispute was lost or accepted |
| `payment_method.attached` | payment method | A card was saved for a customer |
| `payment_method.updated` | payment method | A saved card was updated |
| `payment_method.detached` | payment method | A saved card was removed |

## Fulfilment rule of thumb

Fulfil on `payment.captured` when `amount_captured` equals your order total, `currency`
matches and `amount_mismatch` is `false`. With manual capture, ship on
`payment.authorized` and capture when you ship.
